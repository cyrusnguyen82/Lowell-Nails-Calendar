# Workflow: Lead Generation

**Objective:** Generate a steady, predictable flow of new booking leads through organic and paid channels.

**Inputs required:** Google Business Profile access, Meta Business Manager access (optional), website with booking widget embedded

**Outputs:** New leads entering the booking widget or contacting the business directly

---

## Channels (Priority Order)

### 1. Google Business Profile (Free — Highest ROI)

**Setup (one-time):**
- Verify the business at Google Business Profile
- Upload at least 10 photos (before/after, team, workspace)
- Fill every field: hours, services, description, website link
- Add the booking widget URL to the "Book" button

**Ongoing (weekly):**
- Post one update (promotion, tip, or result photo) every 7 days
- Respond to every review within 24 hours
- Add new photos monthly

**Why this works:** 46% of all Google searches are local. A fully optimized GBP appears in the local 3-pack and drives zero-cost inbound traffic.

---

### 2. Booking Widget SEO Page

**Setup (one-time):**
- Create a dedicated landing page on the website per service category
- Title format: "[Service] in [City] — Book Online"
- Each page should include: service description, pricing, photos, FAQ, embedded booking widget
- Submit sitemap to Google Search Console

**Ongoing:**
- Update pages when services or pricing change
- Add new FAQ entries based on questions clients actually ask

---

### 3. Short-Form Video (Instagram Reels / TikTok)

**Posting cadence:** 3x per week minimum

**Content types that convert:**
- Before/after transformations (highest engagement)
- "Day in the life" behind the scenes
- Quick tip or tutorial (positions you as an expert)
- Client reaction / testimonial clip
- Trending audio with results overlay

**CTA on every post:** "Book the link in bio" or "DM to book"

**Link in bio:** Direct to booking widget URL

---

### 4. Meta Ads (Paid — Start Month 2)

**When to start:** After organic is producing at least 5 bookings/month. Don't pay to acquire leads before you know you can convert them.

**Campaign structure:**

| Campaign | Audience | Budget | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retargeting | Website visitors (pixel required) | $5/day | Rebooking |
| Lookalike | 1% lookalike of best clients | $10/day | New acquisition |
| Local awareness | 5-mile radius, age 18-55 | $5/day | Brand |

**Creative:** Use best-performing organic Reels as ad creative. Native-looking content outperforms produced ads.

**Install pixel:** Add Meta pixel to website before running any paid ads. This is non-negotiable.

---

### 5. Google Search Ads (Paid — Start Month 3)

**When to start:** Once you know your cost per acquisition from Meta.

**Keywords to target:**
- "[Service] near me"
- "[Service] in [City]"
- "Best [service] [city]"
- "[Competitor name] alternative" (if applicable)

**Budget:** Start at $15/day. Scale only if cost per booking is under your acceptable CPA.

**Landing page:** Send clicks to a dedicated service page with the booking widget, not the homepage.

---

## Lead Capture Rules

1. **Every lead must have a next step.** Website visitors who don't book should hit an email capture (lead magnet: "Book your free consultation" or "Get our service menu").
2. **Speed to lead.** If someone fills out a contact form, respond within 5 minutes. Response time is the #1 conversion driver.
3. **Every social DM gets a response within 1 hour** during business hours.

---

## Edge Cases

- **Low traffic:** If the booking widget gets fewer than 50 visits/month, fix organic discovery first (GBP, SEO) before spending on ads.
- **High traffic, low bookings:** The problem is conversion, not traffic. Audit the booking flow for friction.
- **Seasonal dips:** Run a limited-time offer 2 weeks before historically slow periods. See `templates/email_nurture_sequence.md` for promo email templates.
