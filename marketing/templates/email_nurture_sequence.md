# Email Nurture Sequence — Full Copy

## Sequence A: New Lead (Never Booked)

---

### Email 1 — Welcome (Send immediately)

**Subject:** Welcome — here's what to expect

**Body:**
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> Thanks for your interest in {{business_name}}. We're glad you found us.
>
> Here's what we want you to know upfront: we take our work seriously, we keep things on schedule, and we want every client to leave feeling like it was worth it.
>
> Over the next few days I'll share a few things that'll help you decide if we're the right fit. No pressure — just real information.
>
> If you have any questions before then, just reply to this email. I read every one.
>
> — {{staff_name}}
