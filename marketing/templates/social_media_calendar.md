# Social Media Content Calendar — 30-Day Template

**Posting cadence:** 5x per week (Mon–Fri), 1 Reel or video per week minimum
**Platforms:** Instagram (primary), TikTok (repost), Facebook (auto-publish from IG)
**Tone:** Real, warm, expert — never salesy

Replace `[YOUR_SERVICE]`, `[YOUR_CITY]`, `[BOOKING_LINK]` with actuals before scheduling.

---

## Content Type Definitions

| Type | Format | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Result** | Before/after photo or video | Proof of work |
| **Reel** | 15-60 sec video | Reach + discovery |
| **Education** | Carousel or single image + caption | Authority + saves |
| **Personality** | Behind the scenes, team, humor | Trust + follows |
| **Social Proof** | Testimonial screenshot or quote card | Conversion |
| **Offer** | Promotional post | Direct bookings |

Keep the ratio: 60% value/personality, 30% proof/social proof, 10% direct offer.

## Week 1

## Hashtag Strategy

**Use a mix of sizes on every post:**

| Type | Examples | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Business-specific | #[BusinessName] #[BusinessNameNails | Small — owned |
| Service-specific | #[ServiceType] #[ServiceType][City] | Medium |
| Local | #[City] #[City][Service] #[CityLife] | Medium |
| Discovery | #[ServiceType]Inspo #[ServiceType]Transformation | Large |
| Trending | Check Instagram Explore weekly — rotate 2-3 trending tags | Large |

Cap at 15-20 hashtags per post. More does not mean more reach.

---

## Caption Formula That Converts

```
Hook (first line — make them stop scrolling)
↓
Body (deliver value, tell the story, or show the result)
↓
Soft CTA (natural next step — not a shout)
↓
Hashtags (first comment or end of caption)
```

**Hook formulas:**
- "Nobody talks about this, but..."
- "The transformation that took [X] hours"
- "3 things every [target client] should know"
- "We almost didn't post this one"
- "This is what [result] actually looks like"

## Monthly Content Audit (Last Day of Month)

1. Pull top 3 posts by reach and saves — do more of whatever format/topic those were
2. Pull bottom 3 posts — understand why they underperformed (wrong time? weak hook? wrong content type?)
3. Check follower growth: is it positive?
4. Check profile link clicks: are people actually going to book?
5. Adjust next month's ratio of content types based on data
