# Referral Program — Outreach Templates

Variables: `{{first_name}}`, `{{business_name}}`, `{{staff_name}}`, `{{referral_code}}`, `{{booking_link}}`, `{{reward_amount}}`, `{{new_client_offer}}`

---

## In-Person Ask (Verbal Script)

Use immediately after a great result, while the client is still expressing satisfaction:

> "I'm so glad you love it — honestly, hearing that makes my day. Hey, if you know anyone who'd enjoy this, I'd love to meet them. If they mention your name when they book, I'll put ${{reward_amount}} credit on your account. Your code is {{referral_code}} — easy to remember."

**Follow-up:** Hand them a physical referral card or text them the link while they're still in front of you.

---

## Partner / Cross-Referral Outreach (Business-to-Business)

Use when approaching complementary local businesses (e.g., a salon approaching a gym, a spa approaching a chiropractor):

> Hey [Business Owner Name],
>
> I'm {{staff_name}}, owner of {{business_name}}. We serve a lot of the same clients you do — [describe overlap] — and I think there's a natural opportunity to refer each other.
>
> Here's what I'm proposing: when one of your clients asks about [your service category], you mention us. When our clients ask about [their service], we send them your way.
>
> No money changes hands — just two local businesses taking care of shared clients.
>
> Would love to grab coffee and talk it through. When works for you?
>
> — {{staff_name}}
> {{business_name}}
