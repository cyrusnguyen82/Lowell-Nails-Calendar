# Reactivation Campaign — Full Copy

Variables: `{{first_name}}`, `{{business_name}}`, `{{staff_name}}`, `{{booking_link}}`, `{{offer}}`, `{{expiry_date}}`

---

## At-Risk Nudge (45-60 days inactive)

**Channel:** Email or SMS
**Offer:** None — relationship-first

---

### Email Version

**Subject:** It's been a minute, {{first_name}}

**Body:**
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> We've been thinking about you — it's been a while since your last visit.
>
> No pressure to book right now, just wanted to say hi and let you know we're here when you're ready.
>
> A few things have changed since your last visit: **[1 genuine update — new staff member, new service, new hours, etc.]**
>
> Hope you're doing well. See you soon.
>
> — {{staff_name}} at {{business_name}}
>
> Ready to book: {{booking_link}}

---

### SMS Version

> Hey {{first_name}}! It's been a bit since we've seen you at {{business_name}}. Hope you're doing great — we're here whenever you're ready to come back. {{booking_link}}

## Dormant Win-Back (91-180 days inactive)

**Channel:** Email + SMS + personal outreach for high-value clients
**Offer:** 20% off or complimentary add-on, 7-day expiry

---

### Email Version

**Subject:** We'd love to earn your trust back

**Body:**
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> I'll be honest — it's been a long time, and I'd rather reach out directly than pretend you haven't been missed.
>
> We've worked hard to keep raising our standards since your last visit. **[One specific improvement — new technique, new product, extended hours, new team member, etc.]**
>
> I want to offer you something to make your return worth it: **{{offer}}** on your next visit.
>
> This offer is just for you, and it expires in 7 days: {{expiry_date}}.
>
> → {{booking_link}}
>
> No obligation, no hard feelings either way. But we'd genuinely love to have you back.
>
> — {{staff_name}}

---

### SMS Version (Personal — Send Manually for High-LTV Clients)

> Hey {{first_name}}, it's {{staff_name}} from {{business_name}}. I wanted to personally reach out — it's been a while and I'd love to have you back. I can offer you {{offer}} on your next visit. Want me to grab you a spot? Just reply here.

## Cold Hail Mary (180+ days inactive)

**Channel:** Email only
**Offer:** Best available offer
**Goal:** Either win them back or confirm they've churned (so you stop contacting them)

---

### Email Version

**Subject:** One last note before we say goodbye

**Body:**
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> I know it's been a very long time, and I don't want to keep showing up in your inbox if we're not the right fit anymore.
>
> So this is the last email I'll send unless you'd like to hear from us.
>
> If you do want to come back, we're making it as easy as possible: **{{offer}}** — our best offer, no conditions.
>
> → {{booking_link}}
>
> If you'd rather we stop emailing, just click unsubscribe below. No hard feelings — we genuinely appreciate the time you spent with us.
>
> And if life just got busy and the timing wasn't right — we understand completely. We'd love to see you when it is.
>
> — {{staff_name}} & the {{business_name}} team

## Post-Reactivation: Rebook Lock-In

When a dormant or cold client returns, send this within 24 hours:

**Subject:** So glad you came back, {{first_name}}

**Body:**
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> It was so great seeing you again today. Genuinely.
>
> We'd love to make sure this time you don't slip through the cracks (on our end or yours). Want me to go ahead and book your next visit now while you're thinking about it?
>
> → {{booking_link}}
>
> Even if you don't book today — we're glad you're back.
>
> — {{staff_name}}
