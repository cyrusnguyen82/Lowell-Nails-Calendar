---

## Benchmarks to Compare Against

| Line Item | Healthy % of Net Revenue |
|---|---|
| Payroll / Commissions | 35-45% |
| Rent | 8-12% |
| Supplies / COGS | 5-10% |
| Marketing | 3-8% |
| Software | 1-3% |
| All other expenses | <10% |
| **Net Profit Target** | **20-30%+** |
