# Weekly Cash Flow Summary Template

Use this to track cash position week by week.
The automated tool generates this — use this format for manual review or sharing with a partner/accountant.

---

## Cash Reserve Targets

| Business Size (Monthly Revenue) | Minimum Reserve |
|---|---|
| Under $5K/month | $5,000 |
| $5K-$15K/month | 2 months expenses |
| $15K-$30K/month | 3 months expenses |
| Over $30K/month | 3 months expenses + payroll buffer |
